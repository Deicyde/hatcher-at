# Integer powers define continuous self-maps of the circle

The completed Hatcher.diskBoundaryTwoHomeomorphCircle has source TopCat.diskBoundary 2, definitionally the standard TopCat.sphere 1. Reuse Hatcher.Circle.degreeMap for natural powers and Mathlib's continuous_zpow for the integer extension. Mathlib/Analysis/Complex/Circle.lean gives coe_zpow and coe_inv_eq_conj. Do not introduce a competing sphere or circle model.

Hatcher.Circle.degreeMap is defined in Hatcher/VanKampen/CyclicPresentationAlgebra.lean and owned by the circle-degree-map article. The statement dependency reaches that article so the positive-restriction identity can use the existing constructor; it does not require the fundamental-group compatibility theorem to construct integer powers.
