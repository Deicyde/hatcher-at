# The signed pinch map has the prescribed local degrees

For y ≠ b and the ith point of the regular fiber of fσ, derive an isolation witness and prove that its public local degree is σᵢ. The equality holds for every witness and uses the existing signed local-degree convention.

## Proof sketch

On Bᵢ the map has the same germ as aᵢ ∘ cᵢ. This corrected component collapse has a singleton fiber over y, so its local degree is its already computed global degree σᵢ. Germ invariance transfers the result to fσ.
