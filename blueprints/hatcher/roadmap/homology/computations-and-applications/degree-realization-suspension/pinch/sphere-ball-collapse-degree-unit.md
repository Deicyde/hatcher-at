# Each ball-collapse map has degree plus or minus one

For every component collapse cᵢ of the sphere pinch map, prove degree(cᵢ) = 1 or degree(cᵢ) = −1. Its sign is the signed integer of the existing ordered sphere orientation, not an unspecified choice of generator.

## Proof sketch

Choose a target y other than b. The punctured-sphere chart gives exactly one preimage, in Bᵢ, and a genuine local homeomorphism there. The local degree is a unit, and the singleton-fiber comparison identifies it with the global degree. Do not assume that the chart is positively oriented.
