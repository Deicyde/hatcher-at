# Collapsing a compact complement gives a one-point compactification

For a compact Hausdorff space X and an open subspace V with nonempty complement, construct a homeomorphism X/(X−V) ≃ OnePoint(V). It sends the collapsed complement to infinity and identifies the actual quotient projection on V with the canonical inclusion into the one-point compactification.

## Proof sketch

Use the quotient topology, the embedding of V away from the collapsed point, and the compact-neighborhood characterization at infinity. The homeomorphism must identify the specified projection, not merely show that the two spaces are abstractly homeomorphic. Allow V to be empty.
