# Regular pinch fibers are indexed by the chosen balls

For the signed pinch-and-fold map fσ and every y ≠ b, construct an equivalence fσ⁻¹({y}) ≃ Fin k. The point indexed by i lies in Bᵢ and is the inverse image of aᵢ⁻¹(y) under that ball's punctured-sphere chart.

## Proof sketch

Each correction fixes b, so its inverse preserves the regular target locus. Outside the balls the map is b, and disjointness prevents repeated preimages. The equivalence derives fiber finiteness and includes the empty fiber when k = 0.
