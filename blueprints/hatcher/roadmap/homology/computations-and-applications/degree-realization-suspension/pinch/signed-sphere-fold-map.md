# Reflections correct the signs of a sphere fold map

For prescribed σᵢ ∈ {1,−1}, construct a based fold pσ : ⋁ₖ Sⁿ → Sⁿ and the continuous self-map fσ = pσ ∘ q. On the ith summand choose identity or the zeroth-coordinate reflection, which fixes b, according to the proved sign of cᵢ.

## Proof sketch

Let aᵢ be this correction. The restriction of fσ to Bᵢ agrees with aᵢ ∘ cᵢ, and degree(aᵢ ∘ cᵢ) = σᵢ follows from multiplicativity and the reflection calculation. The choice is made from the computed unit degree; no prescribed-degree equation is assumed as construction data. For k = 0 the fold gives the constant map to b.
