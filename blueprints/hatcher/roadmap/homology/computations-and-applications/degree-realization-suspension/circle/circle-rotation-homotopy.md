# Every circle rotation is homotopic to the identity

For every r on the complex unit circle, let R_r be the transported rotation z ↦ r z of S¹. Construct a homotopy from the identity to R_r and deduce degree(R_r) = 1.

## Proof sketch

Choose a real argument θ of r and multiply by exp(i t θ), for t ∈ I. This is a homotopy of actual circle self-maps with the required endpoints. The choice of θ occurs only inside the construction and places no additional hypothesis on the rotation.
