# Integer powers define continuous self-maps of the circle

Using the completed homeomorphism e between the standard S¹ and the complex unit circle, define P_d(x) = e⁻¹((e x)^d) for every integer d. Construct this actual continuous sphere self-map from the continuous integer power operation.

## Proof sketch

Record its complex-coordinate equation, P₀ being constant, and the negative-exponent identity P_{−k} = P_{−1} ∘ P_k for k : ℕ. The positive restriction reuses the existing circle power map rather than redefining its underlying operation. This fixes the circle model for the degree calculation.
