# Positive circle powers stretch local angular charts

For k > 0 and x ∈ S¹, construct open arc neighborhoods of x and P_k(x) on which P_k is a homeomorphism. In centered complex angular coordinates x exp(i t), the map is x^k exp(i k t).

## Proof sketch

Take a sufficiently short open interval about zero so that both exponential charts are injective. Scaling by k gives the interval homeomorphism and proves that x is an isolated preimage of P_k(x). Use local arcs, not a purported global homeomorphism from the circle to its arguments.
