# The degree of an integer circle power is its exponent

**Hatcher, Example 2.32.** For every d : ℤ, prove degree(P_d) = d for the actual map z ↦ z^d on the complex circle transported to S¹.

## Proof sketch

Use the positive-power calculation when d > 0. For d < 0, use the negative-exponent composition equation and the inversion/reflection degree −1. When d = 0, use the constant-map equation and nonsurjective-degree theorem, not the finite-fiber sum: the constant power has an infinite fiber over 1.
