# Every local degree of a positive circle power is plus one

For k > 0 and every x ∈ S¹, derive isolation over P_k(x) and prove localDegree(P_k,x,P_k(x)) = 1, independently of its witness.

## Proof sketch

The short-arc deformation preserves local degree by punctured-homotopy invariance. Its terminal map is the rotation sending x to P_k(x); the local degree of this global homeomorphism equals its global degree, which is 1 by the rotation homotopy. A local homeomorphism alone would only give an unspecified sign.
