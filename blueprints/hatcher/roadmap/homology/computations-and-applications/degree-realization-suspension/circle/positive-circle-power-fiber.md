# A positive circle power has exactly as many preimages as its exponent

For k > 0 and every y ∈ S¹, construct an equivalence P_k⁻¹({y}) ≃ Fin k. In complex coordinates its points are the actual kth roots of e(y), so fiber finiteness is derived rather than assumed.

## Proof sketch

Choose one root and multiply it by all kth roots of unity, or use the finite covering's fiber description. Check that every root lies on the unit circle and that the parametrization is injective and exhaustive. No assertion about the fiber of the zero power is made.
