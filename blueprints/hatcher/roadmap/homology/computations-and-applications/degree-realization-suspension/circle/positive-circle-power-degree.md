# A positive circle power has its exponent as degree

For every natural k > 0, prove degree(P_k) = (k : ℤ).

## Proof sketch

Apply Proposition 2.30 to the fiber over any target. Its equivalence with Fin k gives exactly k summands, and each local degree is +1. Keep the actual power map and the source's signed orientation convention.
