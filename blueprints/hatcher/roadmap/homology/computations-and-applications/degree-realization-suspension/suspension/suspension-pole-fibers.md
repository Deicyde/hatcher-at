# The suspension poles have singleton fibers

For every n : ℕ and every continuous f : Sⁿ → Sⁿ, prove that Sf⁻¹({N}) = {N} and Sf⁻¹({S}) = {S}, where N and S are the existing north and south poles of Sⁿ⁺¹.

## Proof sketch

The retained cylinder height is unchanged by Cf. The quotient coordinate formulas show that reaching either pole forces its endpoint class. No injectivity, surjectivity, or nonconstancy condition is imposed on f.
