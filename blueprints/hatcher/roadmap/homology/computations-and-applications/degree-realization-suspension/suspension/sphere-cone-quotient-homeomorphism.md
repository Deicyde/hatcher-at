# The cone-base quotient is the next sphere with its suspension poles

Construct a fixed homeomorphism eₙ : CSⁿ/Sⁿ ≃ Sⁿ⁺¹ for every n : ℕ. In the standard Euclidean coordinates it sends the class of (x,r) to (sqrt(1−(2r−1)²) x, 2r−1). The collapsed retained base is the existing north pole and the cone apex is the existing south pole.

## Proof sketch

Descend the continuous cylinder formula through both quotients. Away from the poles its inverse recovers the last coordinate and normalizes the remaining vector. Prove bijectivity and use compactness and Hausdorffness to obtain the homeomorphism. These formulas must include n = 0 and both endpoint classes.
