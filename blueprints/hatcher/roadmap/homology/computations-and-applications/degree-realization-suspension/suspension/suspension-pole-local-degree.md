# The local degree at a suspension pole is its global degree

For every n : ℕ, continuous f : Sⁿ → Sⁿ, and either suspension pole p, derive that p is an isolated preimage of itself under Sf and prove localDegree(Sf,p,p) = degree(Sf), for every isolation witness.

## Proof sketch

Apply the shared singleton-fiber degree comparison in the positive dimension n+1 to the exact pole-fiber equations. The suspension need not be a local homeomorphism at a pole. This statement is valid also for the suspension of a map of S⁰.
