# The suspension boundary isomorphism intertwines the induced maps

For every n : ℕ, construct the canonical integral isomorphism Δₙ : H̃ₙ₊₁(Sⁿ⁺¹) ≃ H̃ₙ(Sⁿ) obtained by the good-pair quotient comparison and the boundary map of the contractible cone-base pair. For every f prove the source diagram: Δₙ ∘ (Sf)∗ = f∗ ∘ Δₙ.

## Proof sketch

The forward map must be the quotient-to-relative comparison followed by the actual connecting map, transported through eₙ. Prove its invertibility from the cone geometry and derive naturality from the actual Cf square and quotient-sequence naturality. Use reduced homology also at n = 0; ordinary H₀(S⁰) is not an infinite cyclic group.
