# CW-subcomplex regular neighborhoods

Hatcher's Appendix Proposition A.5 constructs an open regular neighborhood
of every subcomplex of a CW complex and strongly deformation retracts that
neighborhood onto the subcomplex. The construction here is deliberately
narrow: it fixes one common collar width for every cell and retains the
intersection identity needed for Corollary 2.24.
