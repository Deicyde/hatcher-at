# Post-excision applications

Hatcher §2.1, printed pages 125–126. This milestone begins immediately after
Proposition 2.22 and uses the completed relative-homology, excision, good-pair,
and sphere-homology APIs. It covers the arbitrary-pair mapping-cone
comparison, the explicit fundamental cycles of Example 2.23, CW-subcomplex
excision and wedge additivity in Corollaries 2.24–2.25, and invariance of
dimension in Theorem 2.26.

The milestone has twenty-seven §2.1 leaves. Five separate Appendix leaves
construct the Proposition A.5 regular neighborhoods needed by Corollary 2.24.
Theorem 2.27, degree, cellular homology, and homology with coefficients remain
outside this boundary.
