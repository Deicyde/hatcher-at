# Degree foundations and applications

Hatcher §2.2, printed pages 134–135. This milestone defines the integral
degree of a self-map of a positive-dimensional sphere from the completed
ordered sphere class, proves properties (a)–(g), and applies them to tangent
vector fields and free group actions.

The construction deliberately uses the first-simplex-minus-second-simplex
orientation from Example 2.23. Reflections are computed by exchanging those
two simplices, so the roadmap does not depend on the deferred
simplicial–singular comparison theorem.

All sixteen formalization leaves are formalized locally and complete.
