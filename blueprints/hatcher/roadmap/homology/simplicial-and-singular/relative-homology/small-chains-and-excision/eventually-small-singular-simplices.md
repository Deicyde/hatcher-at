# Iterated subdivision eventually makes every singular simplex small

Let `U : ι → Set X` be a family whose interiors cover `X`. Define the
simplicial subcomplex of the singular set generated by simplices whose images
lie in one `U i`, and say that a simplex is small after `k` subdivisions when
every permutation-indexed subsimplex of that iterate belongs to this
subcomplex.

The main theorem, intended as
`Hatcher.Excision.exists_iteratedSubdivision_mem_smallSubcomplex`, says that
every singular simplex is small after some finite number of subdivisions.
Apply the pinned compact-space Lebesgue-number lemma to the inverse-image cover
of the standard simplex and combine it with the diameter-decay estimate. The
subdivision depth is allowed to depend on the simplex.
