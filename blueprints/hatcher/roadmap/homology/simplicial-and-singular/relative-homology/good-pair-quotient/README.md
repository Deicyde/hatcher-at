# Good pairs and quotient exact sequences

Hatcher §2.1, Theorem 2.13 and Proposition 2.22 (printed pages 114,
124, and 128). For a good pair `(X,A)`, the quotient map identifies relative
homology with reduced homology of `X/A`. Transporting the reduced pair sequence
through this comparison gives the quotient long exact sequence, naturally in
maps of good pairs.

The point quotient is represented functorially as the TopCat pushout
`X ⊔_A PUnit`. The good-pair witness keeps Hatcher's exact neighborhood
hypothesis `A ⊆ interior V`; it does not assume that the chosen `V` is open.
The topology and homology bridges are decomposed separately so that the final
source-facing theorems use canonical quotient-induced maps.
