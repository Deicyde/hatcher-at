# The cellular complex is formed from skeletal relative homology

For every classical CW complex X, construct its ordinary integral cellular
chain complex with Cₙ = Hₙ(Xⁿ,Xⁿ⁻¹;ℤ). Its differential from Cₙ₊₁ to Cₙ is
the connecting map for (Xⁿ⁺¹,Xⁿ), followed by the relative projection for
(Xⁿ,Xⁿ⁻¹). Record this formula for the actual maps and prove consecutive
differentials compose to zero. The outgoing degree-zero differential is zero.

## Proof sketch

The two middle maps in a composite differential are successive maps of the
actual pair long exact sequence, so their composite vanishes. Use the
standard natural-number chain-complex shape, without an augmentation.
