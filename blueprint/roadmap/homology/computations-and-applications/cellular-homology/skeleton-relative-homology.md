# Skeletal relative homology is concentrated in the cell dimension

**Hatcher, Lemma 2.34(a).** For every classical CW complex X and n,k ∈ ℕ,
Hₖ(Xⁿ,Xⁿ⁻¹;ℤ) is zero when k ≠ n and is the free abelian group on the actual
n-cells when k = n. Construct the degree-n isomorphism so each standard basis
vector goes to the corresponding characteristic-map fundamental class.
Here X⁻¹ is empty; empty complexes and infinite cell families are included.

## Proof sketch

Combine the ordinary zero-skeleton calculation with the positive-dimensional
skeletal-pair calculation, preserving their actual cell-generator maps.
