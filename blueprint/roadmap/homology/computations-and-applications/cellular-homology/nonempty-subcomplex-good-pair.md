# A nonempty CW subcomplex gives a good pair

For any subcomplex A of a classical CW complex X with A nonempty, the actual
pair (X,A), with its subspace inclusion, is a good pair. Its neighborhood and
strong deformation retraction are constructed from the CW structure.

## Proof sketch

Use the canonical regular neighborhood of A and its strong deformation
retraction. A is closed in X and lies in the interior of that neighborhood;
the existing inclusion equations give the required deformation onto A.
