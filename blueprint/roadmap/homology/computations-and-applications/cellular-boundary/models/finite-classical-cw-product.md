# Finite classical CW complexes have their actual product cell structure

For finite classical CW complexes X and Y, construct a finite classical CW structure on their ordinary topological product. Its cells are actual products of factor cells, with dimensions p+q and product characteristic-map equations.

## Proof sketch

Concatenate classical sup-norm cell coordinates; their disks and frontiers become the product disks and boundary union. Use the finite classical constructor and the actual product topology.
