# Projective points are unit vectors modulo phase

For every n ∈ ℕ, construct a homeomorphism from ℂPⁿ to the unit sphere for the standard Hermitian norm in ℂⁿ⁺¹ modulo unit complex scalars. Its inverse sends a unit vector to its actual complex line; preserve both quotient-map equations.

## Proof sketch

Normalize a nonzero vector by its norm. Nonzero scalar equivalence becomes unit-phase equivalence after normalization, and the normalized inclusion and quotient maps are continuous inverses.
