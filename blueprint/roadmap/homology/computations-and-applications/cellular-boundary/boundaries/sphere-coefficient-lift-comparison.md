# The sphere chain comparison transports coefficients and the fixed class

For every universe u and positive sphere dimension m, let Lᵤ be the canonical
additive coefficient lift from small abelian groups to the chosen larger
category. Construct the augmented chain isomorphism
Lᵤ(C₊(Sᵐ;ℤ)) ≅ C₊(Sᵐᵤ;Lᵤℤ), where Sᵐᵤ is the canonical space lift.
Retain its equations on actual simplex coefficient copies and augmentation,
and prove its image of the fixed ordered fundamental cycle and homology class.

## Proof sketch

Combine literal sphere-simplex transport with the additive coefficient lift and its coproduct comparison. Prove the chain and ordered-cycle equations before passing to homology.
