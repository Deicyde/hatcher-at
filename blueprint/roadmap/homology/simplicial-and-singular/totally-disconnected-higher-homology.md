# Higher homology vanishes for totally disconnected spaces

For a totally disconnected space `X` and `n ≠ 0`, the degree-`n` singular
homology object of `X` is zero. This pinned theorem is stronger than the
positive-degree clause of Hatcher's point calculation and supplies that clause
after specializing `X` to a one-point space.
