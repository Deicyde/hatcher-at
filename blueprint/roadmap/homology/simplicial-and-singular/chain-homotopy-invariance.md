# Chain-homotopic maps induce the same homology map

**Hatcher, Proposition 2.12 (page 113).** If two chain maps are chain
homotopic, their induced morphisms on homology are equal in every degree.

This is exactly the pinned lemma `Homotopy.homologyMap_eq`.
