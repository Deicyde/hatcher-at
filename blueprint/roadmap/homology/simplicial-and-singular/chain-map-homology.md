# A chain map induces a map on homology

**Hatcher, Proposition 2.9 (page 111).** A chain map between chain complexes
induces a morphism between their homology objects in every degree.

The pinned definition `HomologicalComplex.homologyMap` is the categorical
construction. Its companion lemmas `homologyMap_id` and `homologyMap_comp`
give the identity and composition laws used in Corollary 2.11.
