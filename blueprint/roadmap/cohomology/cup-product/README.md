# Cup product

Hatcher §3.2 (pages 206–229). Mapped, not yet decomposed.

The cup product of cochains, defined on singular cochains by splitting a
simplex into front and back faces, descends to cohomology and makes
`H∗(X; R)` a graded ring, graded-commutative when `R` is commutative. The
ring structure is a genuinely finer invariant: `S² ∨ S⁴` and `ℂP²` have the
same cohomology groups and different cohomology rings. The section computes
the rings of projective spaces, proves a Künneth formula for products, and
surveys spaces with polynomial cohomology.

Nothing here is upstream, and it depends on
[cohomology groups](../cohomology-groups/README.md) existing first. The
graded-commutativity proof is the technical centre.
