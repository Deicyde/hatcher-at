# A lift is determined by one point

**Hatcher, Proposition 1.34 (page 62).** Two continuous lifts from a connected
space that agree at one point agree everywhere.

The pinned declaration `IsCoveringMap.eq_of_comp_eq` states this with
`PreconnectedSpace` and a supplied comparison point. The point supplies the
nonemptiness implicit in Hatcher's connectedness hypothesis.
