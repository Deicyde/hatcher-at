# The base-side auxiliary cover retracts onto the original space

Construct a strong deformation retraction from the base-side member of
Hatcher's auxiliary binary cover onto the canonical copy of `X`. The homotopy
first collapses the added strips toward their attached bottom edges and then
pushes all positive-height cone points to their attaching points, while fixing
`X` throughout.

Intended artifact:
`Hatcher.VanKampen.AuxiliaryCellAttachment.baseCoverStrongDeformationRetract`.
