## Lemma 2.34

Printed pages 137–139. Part (a) identifies the relative homology of successive
skeleta with the free group on cells in their dimension, and zero in other
degrees. Part (b) gives vanishing above a skeleton’s dimension and its
finite-dimensional consequence. Part (c) gives the actual skeleton inclusion
isomorphism below that dimension and surjection in that dimension.

The proof uses the good skeletal pair and its sphere wedge quotient,
Proposition 2.22, Corollary 2.25 and pair exactness. For unbounded dimensions,
the selected proof uses finite support of actual singular cycles and bounding
chains, whose compact images lie in finite skeleta by Appendix A.1. The
source’s telescope argument is an alternative, not an extra prerequisite.

## Cellular complex

Printed page 139. The chain group in degree n is the actual relative group of
the n-skeleton and its predecessor. The differential is the pair connecting
map followed by relativization; consecutive differentials vanish by
exactness. Its degree-zero convention is ordinary and unaugmented.

## Theorem 2.35

Statement on printed page 139 and proof on 140. Identify cellular cycles and
boundaries via the relative projection and pair connecting images, and use
the actual skeletal inclusions to obtain the cellular/singular comparison.
The intended formal comparison records its forward-map equation.
