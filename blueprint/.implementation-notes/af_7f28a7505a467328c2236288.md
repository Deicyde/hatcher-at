# Local degree of a positive circle power

**Uncompiled source draft.** The proposed theorem is
`Hatcher.Sphere.circlePowerMap_positive_localDegree` in
`Hatcher/Sphere/HomeomorphismLocalDegree.lean`.

The full-radius local stretching homotopy supplies a jointly continuous map,
its actual power-map and rotation endpoints, center fixing, and every-time
puncture avoidance. `localDegree_punctured_homotopy_invariance` derives actual
isolation for both maps and compares their signed local degrees for every pair
of isolation witnesses.

The local value `E` is the sphere homeomorphism obtained by conjugating circle
left multiplication by `r = e(P_k x) * (e x)⁻¹`. Its induced morphism is
identified with the existing `circleRotation r`; its image of `x` is identified
with `P_k x` using the homotopy's endpoint and center equations.
`localDegree_homeomorph_eq_degree` then computes the rotation's local degree,
and `circleRotation_homotopic_identity_and_degree` supplies its proved global
degree one. The public conclusion includes derived isolation and the equality
for every power-map isolation witness, uniformly including `k = 1`.
