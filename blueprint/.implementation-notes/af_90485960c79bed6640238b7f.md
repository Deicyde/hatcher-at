# Implementation note: Cell counts bound the number of homology generators

## Remaining goal

For every classical CW complex X and n,k ∈ ℕ, if X has exactly k n-cells, Hₙ(X;ℤ) has a generating family of at most k elements. No other cell dimension is required to be finite, and no freeness conclusion for Hₙ is asserted.

## Candidate declaration

`Hatcher.ClassicalCW.cellularGeneratorBound` is a naming proposal, not a verified declaration.

## Route

The cycle group is a subgroup of the rank-k cellular group. Apply the finite free subquotient bound to cycles modulo actual cellular boundaries, then transport by the cellular/singular comparison.

## Existing algebra and cellular APIs

Reuse `Hatcher.ClassicalCW.skeletonRelativeHomology`, `cellularChainComplex`,
`cellularHomologyComparison` and its actual forward map. Mathlib supplies
`ShortComplex.isZero_homology_of_isZero_X₂`, `HomologyData.ofZeros`,
`Submodule.nonempty_basis_of_pid`,
`LinearIndependent.fintype_card_le_finrank`, and
`AddGroup.rank_le_of_surjective`. The PID basis does not alone supply r ≤ k;
prove that bound through the included independent family. The abelian group
rank-monotonicity TODO in `GroupTheory/Rank.lean` is not a theorem.
