# A sphere map induces a self-map of its cone-base pair

The candidate `Hatcher.Sphere.sphereConePairMap n f` uses the existing
`sphereConePair n` for both source and target. Its ambient component is
`sphereConeMap n f`. A private prequotient map applies `f` to the base and
sphere coordinate and fixes the explicit apex and retained height. Its
commutation with `normalForm id` proves preservation of the actual cone
setoid, and `Quotient.map'` supplies the quotient map.

Continuity is proved through the existing `isQuotientMap_quotientMk id` and
the continuous prequotient map. `TopPair.ofHom` uses the actual retained-base
square and the original sphere morphism `f` as its subspace component.
The construction is uniform in every `n : ℕ`, including zero, and in every
continuous sphere map, including constant maps. Preserving `r` preserves
Hatcher's source height `t = 1 - r`.
