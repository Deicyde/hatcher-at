# Implementation note: A finite free abelian subquotient has at most the same number of generators

## Remaining goal

Let F be a free abelian group with a basis of k elements, k ∈ ℕ. For subgroups K ≤ F and L ≤ K, construct a generating family of K/L indexed by at most k elements. Include k = 0; the quotient may have torsion.

## Candidate declaration

`Hatcher.ClassicalCW.freeSubquotientGeneratorBound` is a naming proposal, not a verified declaration.

## Route

Choose the finite integral basis of K supplied by the PID subgroup theorem. Inclusion makes it an independent family in F, so its size is at most k. Pass its generators through the actual quotient map.

## Existing algebra and cellular APIs

Reuse `Hatcher.ClassicalCW.skeletonRelativeHomology`, `cellularChainComplex`,
`cellularHomologyComparison` and its actual forward map. Mathlib supplies
`ShortComplex.isZero_homology_of_isZero_X₂`, `HomologyData.ofZeros`,
`Submodule.nonempty_basis_of_pid`,
`LinearIndependent.fintype_card_le_finrank`, and
`AddGroup.rank_le_of_surjective`. The PID basis does not alone supply r ≤ k;
prove that bound through the included independent family. The abelian group
rank-monotonicity TODO in `GroupTheory/Rank.lean` is not a theorem.
