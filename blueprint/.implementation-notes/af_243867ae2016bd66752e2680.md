# Implementation note: The sphere chain comparison transports coefficients and the fixed class

## Remaining goal

Construct the canonical comparison for integral chains on each positive-dimensional standard sphere and its chosen lifted model with lifted integral coefficients. Retain its actual simplex equations and the image of the fixed ordered fundamental cycle and homology class.

## Candidate declaration

`Hatcher.ClassicalCW.sphereCoefficientLiftComparison` is a naming proposal, not a verified declaration.

## Route

Combine literal sphere-simplex transport with the additive coefficient lift and its coproduct comparison. Prove the chain and ordered-cycle equations before passing to homology.

## Fixed model and API constraints

Keep `positiveStandardDiskFundamentalClass`,
`positiveSkeletonCellFundamentalClass`, `skeletalCellFundamentalClass` and
the actual `cellularDifferential`. Their maps are in
`Hatcher/Singular/PointedWedgeHomology.lean` and
`Hatcher/Singular/Relative.lean`. Natural-number adjacent degrees are
(n+1,n); the outgoing degree-zero map is unaugmented and zero.

## Coefficient and sphere transport

`sphereAugmentedSpaceLiftIso` keeps its coefficient category fixed. The new
comparison must also apply `AddCommGrpCat.uliftFunctor` to the small
coefficient category. That functor is additive, fully faithful and preserves
limits and colimits; use `ShortComplex.mapHomologyIso` and its naturality
with actual simplex/cycle equations. The existing degree is the small-model
integer multiplier. Relate it to the fixed lifted ordered class in positive
degree through the canonical reduced/ordinary comparison.
