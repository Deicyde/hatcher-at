# Positive circle-power angular charts

**UNCOMPILED DRAFT; not accepted formalization.** The proposed declaration is
`Hatcher.Sphere.circlePowerMap_positive_arcChart` in
`Hatcher/Sphere/CirclePower.lean`. All its supporting constructions are local
to the theorem proof.

The source arc is the preimage of `Circle.centeredArc (π / (2k))` under
`z ↦ (e x)⁻¹ * e z`; the target uses center `(e x)^k` and radius `π / 2`.
Restricted exponential charts and positive multiplication supply the actual
homeomorphism. The theorem exposes both chart equations, the scaling map,
commutation with the existing power map, its complex-coordinate equation, and
the singleton central fiber inside the open source arc.

The pinned Mathlib APIs used are `Circle.bijOn_exp_Ioo_centeredArc`,
`Circle.isOpen_centeredArc`, `Circle.isCoveringMap_exp.isOpenMap`,
`Set.BijOn.equiv`, `Equiv.toHomeomorphOfContinuousOpen`,
`Continuous.subtype_map`, `IsOpenMap.subtype_map`, `Homeomorph.subtype`,
`Homeomorph.mulLeft`, `Homeomorph.mulLeft₀`, and `Circle.exp_natCast_mul`.
