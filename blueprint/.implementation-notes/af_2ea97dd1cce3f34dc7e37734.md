# Implementation note: Cellular boundaries follow the characteristic-map connecting square

## Remaining goal

For every n ≥ 1 and actual n-cell α, the cellular differential of its fixed characteristic class is the actual relative projection after the attaching-map image of the ordinary boundary of its fixed disk class. Retain the characteristic-map naturality equation, including the one-dimensional case.

## Candidate declaration

`Hatcher.ClassicalCW.characteristicBoundaryNaturality` is a naming proposal, not a verified declaration.

## Route

The actual characteristic and attaching maps form a map of pairs. Apply ordinary connecting-map naturality and the defining formula for the cellular differential.

## Fixed model and API constraints

Keep `positiveStandardDiskFundamentalClass`,
`positiveSkeletonCellFundamentalClass`, `skeletalCellFundamentalClass` and
the actual `cellularDifferential`. Their maps are in
`Hatcher/Singular/PointedWedgeHomology.lean` and
`Hatcher/Singular/Relative.lean`. Natural-number adjacent degrees are
(n+1,n); the outgoing degree-zero map is unaugmented and zero.

## Connecting square

`pairConnecting_naturality` and `skeletalStageCellPairMap` give the actual
characteristic/attaching square. Combine it with the current disk-class image
and the actual relative projection; the statement includes n = 1.
