## Candidate

and independent review. It constructs the actual degree-n cellular chain

canonical cycle inclusion and homology quotient. The resulting free-cell

## Route

Use `cellularDifferentialsZeroOfSparseCells` for the actual predecessor and
successor selected by the chain-complex shape. Mathlib's `iCyclesIso` and
`isoHomologyπ` identify cycles with the chain group and with homology using
their canonical maps. Compose their isomorphisms, then the fixed basis from
`skeletonRelativeHomology` and `cellularHomologyComparison`. All natural
indices, arbitrary cell sets, empty complexes and disconnected carriers are
included, with no added finiteness or positive-degree hypothesis.
