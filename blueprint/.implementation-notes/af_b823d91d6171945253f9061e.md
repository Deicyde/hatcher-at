# Implementation note: Gaps between cell dimensions give the cell basis in homology

## Remaining goal

For every classical CW complex X with no adjacent occupied cell dimensions and every n ∈ ℕ, construct Hₙ(X;ℤ) as the free abelian group on the actual n-cells. Preserve those cell generators through the cellular/singular comparison. Include arbitrary cell sets, disconnected spaces and empty complexes.

## Candidate declaration

`Hatcher.ClassicalCW.sparseCellularHomology` is a naming proposal, not a verified declaration.

## Route

With both neighboring differentials zero, cellular homology is the actual degree-n chain group. Transport its fixed cell basis by the completed comparison.

## Existing algebra and cellular APIs

Reuse `Hatcher.ClassicalCW.skeletonRelativeHomology`, `cellularChainComplex`,
`cellularHomologyComparison` and its actual forward map. Mathlib supplies
`ShortComplex.isZero_homology_of_isZero_X₂`, `HomologyData.ofZeros`,
`Submodule.nonempty_basis_of_pid`,
`LinearIndependent.fintype_card_le_finrank`, and
`AddGroup.rank_le_of_surjective`. The PID basis does not alone supply r ≤ k;
prove that bound through the included independent family. The abelian group
rank-monotonicity TODO in `GroupTheory/Rank.lean` is not a theorem.
