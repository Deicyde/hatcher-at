the global open embedding, pairwise disjoint closed images, closed

openness, and homeomorphisms from the closed balls and to the open balls.

## Proof route

Distinct natural-number indices differ by at least one. Their centers
therefore have distance at least four, exceeding the sum of the unit
radii. Inverse stereographic projection followed by the open inclusion of
the punctured sphere gives an open embedding into the actual standard
sphere. Compactness of the Euclidean closed balls makes their sphere
images closed.

The open-map preimage formula for interiors and injectivity identify each
literal sphere interior with the image of its Euclidean open ball. The
embedding's `homeomorphImage` supplies both ball charts. Orthogonality of
the first and last basis vectors proves the axial point's reflection
equation.
